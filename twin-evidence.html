Is the confidence band honest?

We publish a P10 to P90 window, so roughly 80% of real events should land inside it. More than that and the band is too wide to be useful. Fewer and we are overconfident.

79% inside the band 14% earlier than predicted 7% later

What we get wrong, and why

Two of our misses share a cause: both instruments had a consumable changed without being logged, so the twin was reasoning from a wear state that no longer matched reality. That is a workflow gap, not a model gap, and it is why consumable logging is now part of onboarding.

The two false alarms were power events, not machine faults. A supply dip raised current across a whole rack, which the twin read as load. Rack-level correlation was added on 2 August and has suppressed both patterns since.
